The Mechanical Queen

In a post-apocalyptic era where humanity has vanished, the ruins of civilization are claimed by apex machine intelligences and mutated predators. At the apex sits The Queen: a colossal spider-like biomechanical furnace that devours scrap metal and high-tech components to fabricate new scavenger units.

You and your crew were spawned for a single purpose: venture into dark, unpowered complexes, gather abandoned human artifacts, and drag them into her fiery jaws. If your tally falls short of her quota, she won't starve—she'll consume you instead.

ENGINEERED FOR PHYSICS & FUN

Core Gameplay Systems

Custom built in Unity 6 with advanced math, Inverse Kinematics, and networked physics.

SURFACE NORMAL PHYSICS

360° Omnidirectional Climbing

Standard Unity gravity is disabled in favor of a dynamic custom gravity vector. Using raycast dot-product analysis (`EvaluateCollision` & `SnapToGround`), your robot crawls effortlessly across ceilings, pipes, and vertical walls.

  • ✓ Continuous surface normal alignment
  • ✓ Smooth pitch & roll compensation
  • ✓ Toggleable Static vs Dynamic camera mode
RAYCAST & IK

Procedural 8-Legged Body Animation

Rather than canned walk animations, all 8 spider legs use Inverse Kinematics and raycasting to automatically plant their tips on uneven ground, moving along smooth sinusoidal arcs (`sin(t * π) * 0.3`).

  • ✓ Adaptive terrain conformity
  • ✓ Alternating 4-leg gait synchronization
  • ✓ Dynamic step height offsets
PHYSICS SIMULATION

Suction & Elastic Tether Dragging

Ditch traditional grid inventories for tactile physical looting. Activate suction fields to attract nearby salvage, or attach network-synced elastic tethers to drag heavy loads behind you.

  • ✓ Real-time weight vs speed penalty
  • ✓ Network-synced spring joints
  • ✓ Physics knockbacks & collisions
FINITE STATE MACHINE

Hostile AI State Architecture

Dungeon predators operate under an autonomous Server-Authoritative FSM with distinct Idle, Patrol, Chase, and Attack states, leveraging dynamic vision cones and NavMesh navigation.

  • ✓ Sightline & angle detection math
  • ✓ NavMesh pathfinding across rooms
  • ✓ Server-validated damage RPCs

Tactical Physical Looting

Tethering high-tier scrap yields massive credits for the Queen, but the additional weight slows your sprint speed—making stealth and team coordination crucial.

ENGINEERING & ACADEMIC DOSSIER

Technical Architecture

Developed by Emrys Melissant as a Bachelor Project for Multimedia & Creative Technologies (MCT) at Hogeschool MCT.

🎮

Unity 6 & HDRP 17.3

Utilizes Unity 6's high-performance runtime with the High Definition Render Pipeline for volumetric lighting, physical shaders, and realistic shadow mapping in dark abandoned facilities.

🌐

Netcode for GameObjects (NGO)

Server-Authoritative multiplayer with Client-Side movement prediction. Remote Procedure Calls (`[ServerRpc]`, `[ClientRpc]`) synchronize physics tethering and scoring safely.

🎙️

Vivox 3D Voice Audio

Integrated spatial 3D proximity voice communication so team members can whisper or shout across procedural corridors with realistic distance attenuation.

🗺️

Runtime NavMesh AI

Dungeon generation constructs modular rooms at runtime, triggering an asynchronous NavMesh surface bake so monster FSM agents navigate smoothly across dynamically generated corridors.
